Welcome to the Entrepreneurship Ecosystem Diagnostic Tool

 

Why this Diagnostic Tool?

Entrepreneurship ecosystems within Higher Education Institutions play an important role in nurturing innovation, supporting startups and equipping students with entrepreneurial mindsets. However, without a clear picture of the standing and effectiveness of an institution’s entrepreneurial ecosystem in relation to proven benchmarks of excellence, it is challenging to realize the full benefits and value of the institution’s entrepreneurial activities.

 

This diagnostic tool helps your institution to:

Assess Your Ecosystem

Understand your institution’s strengths, gaps and opportunities for entrepreneurship development.

Receive Tailored Recommendation

Get actional insights to develop robust support structures for students and faculty entrepreneurship.

Align with Best Practices

Engage with best practices, case studies, mentorship programs and ecosystem networking opportunities.

 

 

An Evidence-Based Approach to Entrepreneurship Ecosystem Development

This Diagnostic Tool is informed by a comprehensive study on exemplar entrepreneurial ecosystems in sub-Saharan African higher education institutions. It evaluates an institution’s delivery of entrepreneurship development through three key pillars:

  • Entrepreneurship Ecosystem Aspirations: Goals and motivations that shape an institution’s entrepreneurial culture.
  • Entrepreneurship Ecosystem Enablers: Resources, infrastructure, and support systems driving entrepreneurship.
  • Higher Education Institution Activities: Programs, curriculum, and initiatives fostering entrepreneurship.
